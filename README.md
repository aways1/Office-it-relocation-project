Office IT Relocation Project

Project Overview

In December, I contributed to the successful IT relocation of the Ambienta office. The project took approximately 3–4 weeks and involved preparing and deploying IT equipment across approximately 50 workstations.

The project required careful planning, organisation, time management and attention to detail to ensure that equipment was prepared and workstations were ready for use within the required deadlines.

My Role

As part of the IT team, I was involved in the preparation and setup of the new office environment.

My responsibilities included:

* Preparing and setting up approximately 50 workstations
* Installing monitor arms and configuring dual-monitor setups
* Setting up PCs and laptops
* Connecting keyboards and mice
* Setting up telephones
* Configuring and preparing three Konica Minolta printers
* Organising equipment and coordinating tasks throughout the relocation
* Ensuring workstations were prepared according to project requirements
* Adapting to changing requirements during the relocation

Workstation Setup

Each workstation generally consisted of:

* PC or laptop
* Two monitors
* Monitor arm
* Keyboard
* Mouse
* Telephone

Approximately 50 desks were prepared as part of the relocation.

Printer Deployment

Three Konica Minolta printers were configured as part of the office setup.

This required coordinating the printer deployment alongside the workstation setup to ensure the new office was equipped with the required printing facilities.

Project Skills

The relocation required several important IT and professional skills:

Time Management

The project was completed over approximately 3–4 weeks, requiring tasks to be prioritised and completed within deadlines.

Multitasking

Multiple workstations and pieces of equipment needed to be prepared simultaneously, requiring effective task management.

Attention to Detail

Each workstation required multiple pieces of equipment to be correctly installed and organised.

Adaptability

The project involved changing requirements and priorities, requiring the ability to adapt and reorganise tasks when necessary.

Team Coordination

Successful completion of the relocation required coordination with other members of the IT team and the wider office relocation process.

Project Outcome

The office relocation was successfully completed, with approximately 50 workstations and three printers prepared as part of the new office environment.

The project provided practical experience in large-scale IT equipment deployment, workplace technology setup, task management and working to deadlines.
